Gravity holding current static, from URDF inertials

Current = torque / Kt with Kt = 0.441 Nm/A (datasheet, unverified on the bench). The tick marks the servo's 350 mA continuous rating; the bar ends at 1000 mA. J1 turns about a vertical axis and never carries gravity. With springs fitted, J2 and J3 show what the servos still have to supply.
