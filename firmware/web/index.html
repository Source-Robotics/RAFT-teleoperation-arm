Records every control tick on the arm for 2 s: the leader's angles and speeds, and the target sent to PAR6 while teleoperating. Filters A and B are the firmware's target filter (Teleop, Filter: a 2nd-order Butterworth at 250 Hz), computed here on the same 250 Hz data, so cutoffs can be compared before choosing one. Network

Live data delay: from the control tick that measured a frame to this page, estimated as half the best round trip plus how much later than the quickest frame it arrived. The page asks the arm only for what is on screen: live frames at the chosen rate (5 a second on Plots and Health), the status when it changes, and these health figures once a second.

Per servo since the start, raw SyncRead misses (before the retry). Silent: nothing came from it, it did not hear the request or could not answer. Garbled: bytes came but not a valid reply: noise or a damaged signal. Gaps: how many reads in a row it missed, 1 / 2-5 / 6+. The arm rides through gaps on the last angle; with torque on, 50 in a row (0.2 s) trip the fault. Flags: the servo's own error byte in valid replies. Retry: answered when asked again. Move the wrist slowly through its range while watching: misses that follow a pose point at a cable.

Friction (holding) is an extra push against gravity, as a share of the gravity load: the servos' gears slip easily when gravity drives them, so gravity alone lets the arm sink. Raise it if joints sink, lower it if they creep up. Save keeps both.

How hard Hold pushes back toward its pose. Raise it in steps; if a joint starts to buzz or oscillate, go back one step. Not saved yet. With the servos' position mode on, the stiffness only applies to a joint near its encoder's wrap point.

      

      
Per joint (Gravity mode)

        
JointGravity ×Damping Nm·s/rad

        
Gravity factor: on top of the gravity compensation, for a joint whose load the CAD misses (it speeds up as it slides). Raise in 0.1 steps until it stays; lower it if it creeps the other way. Damping: a gentle brake on a joint that keeps coasting after you let go. Defaults J5 0.03, J6 0.02, trigger 0.035 (arm_design.h); too much makes it feel sticky. Changes here are not saved.

Real RTI sends PAR6 one target per 4 ms tick, as it asks (40 ms without one and it stops); SOFT_RTI sends fewer and PAR6 fills in between, with more lag.
